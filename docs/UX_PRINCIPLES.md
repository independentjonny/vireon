# UX Principles

Every Vireon screen is an AI workspace. No page should merely display data.

## The Screen Test

Every screen must answer:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- What should I do?
- What happens if I do nothing?
- How much money is involved?
- What is the confidence level?
- What evidence supports this?
- What is the next action?

## Product Principles

1. Every screen must answer a financial question.
2. AI first. Charts second.
3. Everything connects across transactions, cash flow, balance sheet, borrowing, housing, retirement, goals, timeline, and reports.
4. Every recommendation explains why, evidence, confidence, expected outcome, and time required.
5. Never duplicate calculations. The Financial Vault owns verified financial data.
6. Every feature improves net worth, cash flow, borrowing capacity, tax position, financial confidence, or time saved.

## Workspace Patterns

- Cash Flow becomes Cash Flow Intelligence.
- Mortgage becomes Refinancing and Borrowing Intelligence.
- Documents become Complete My Financial Profile.
- Reports become Executive Briefing.
- Goals become Goal Engine.
- Timeline becomes the user's financial history and future projection.

## One-Click Standard

Prefer workflows that remove work:

- Refinancing Wizard instead of Review Mortgage.
- Reduce Monthly Spending instead of Review Cash Flow.
- Complete My Financial Profile instead of Review Documents.
- Prepare Lender Pack instead of Export Files.
