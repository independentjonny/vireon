# Vireon Master Product Vision

Vireon is not a budgeting application. Vireon is an AI Financial Operating System: the single place where a person manages, understands, models, and improves every aspect of their financial life.

The product promise is simple: the user should feel like they have a world-class Chief Financial Officer available 24/7, backed by verified personal financial data.

For implementation sequencing, use `VIREON_PLATFORM_ROADMAP.md`.

## North Star

A new user should be able to upload bank statements, payslips, tax returns, mortgage statements, super statements, and investment records, then receive within minutes:

- A complete Financial Vault.
- A live balance sheet.
- A financial health score.
- A personalised action plan ranked by financial impact.
- A 30-year wealth projection.
- House affordability analysis.
- Retirement forecast.
- Tax optimisation opportunities.
- Refinance opportunities.
- Spending optimisation.
- Goal tracking.
- Automated document organisation.
- An AI CFO that answers using verified user data.
- A continuously updated digital financial twin.

## Operating Principles

Everything revolves around the Financial Vault. Recommendations must connect to transactions, documents, assets, liabilities, goals, investments, subscriptions, tax records, employment, lending, and property.

Every page is an AI workspace. Each page should answer what changed, why it changed, what to do, what happens if the user does nothing, how much money is involved, confidence, evidence, and next action.

Everything links together. Mortgage, refinance, cash flow, borrowing capacity, home purchase, investment planning, retirement, tax, and net worth must not become isolated surfaces.

## Core Systems

- AI Decision Engine 2.0: rank opportunities by expected financial value using impact, probability, confidence, difficulty, time, risk reduction, cash improvement, net worth improvement, borrowing improvement, tax improvement, and life improvement.
- My AI CFO: replace generic chat with Vault-grounded answers for real financial decisions.
- Financial Timeline: chronological history of statements, documents, income changes, milestones, renewals, rates, goals, and major decisions.
- Digital Financial Twin: model what-if scenarios across cash flow, borrowing, retirement, tax, net worth, goals, and timeline.
- Goal Engine: automatically update intelligent goals from transactions and Vault changes.
- Automation Engine: proactive alerts and workflows for rates, insurance, subscriptions, salary, tax documents, cash levels, due dates, affordability, and investment triggers.
- Financial Health Score: one score from cash flow, emergency fund, debt, investments, diversification, tax, insurance, retirement, borrowing, documents, subscriptions, income stability, goals, and net worth trend.
- Money Map: interactive visualization of income, accounts, bills, subscriptions, savings, investments, loans, and net worth.
- Life Planner: continuous plan for children, travel, semi-retirement, relocation, property, business, education, renovation, and family support.
- Refinance Engine: compare mortgage options, estimate savings, prepare lender packs, calculate borrowing, generate refinance packages, and track progress.
- Document Intelligence Engine: parse PDFs into structured income, loan, interest, property, super, tax, insurance, employment, dates, expiry, confidence, and citations.
- Proactive AI: surface daily changes and offer one-click next actions.
- Executive Mode: one focused screen for net worth, cash, borrowing, goals, top actions, upcoming events, and financial health.
- Professional Mode: deeper analytics, audit trail, evidence, confidence, and assumptions for accountants, advisers, brokers, and family offices.

## Experience Standard

Every recommendation must explain why it exists, what evidence supports it, confidence, assumptions, risks, alternative options, expected benefit, supporting transactions, and supporting documents.

Prefer one-click workflows over passive review pages. Examples: Refinancing Wizard, Reduce Monthly Spending, Complete My Financial Profile.

The UI should be dense without clutter, responsive on desktop and mobile, and support universal search, a command palette, pinned AI panel, keyboard shortcuts, dark mode, subtle motion, and cross-page breadcrumbs.
